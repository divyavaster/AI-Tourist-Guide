🏛️ Badami

Badami was an important Chalukyan centre and is famous for its rock-cut cave temples, sandstone hills, Agasthya Tirtha and Badami Fort.

Badami Attractions

Badami Cave Temples

Agasthya Lake

Historic lake surrounded by temples.

Badami Fort

Historic fort on the sandstone hill.

Bhoothnath Temple

Temple near Agasthya Lake.

🛕 Pattadakal

Pattadakal is a major Chalukyan architectural centre on the Malaprabha River.

Pattadakal Attractions

Virupaksha Temple

Large Dravidian-style temple.

Mallikarjuna Temple

Sangameshwara Temple

Early Dravidian-style temple.

Papanatha Temple

Aihole Attractions

Ladh Khan Temple

Ancient rock-cut Shiva temple.
